Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Yamac Bezirgan ("I", "me") handles information on my website (yamacbezirgan.com) and in the mobile apps and games I publish (together, the "Services").

Information Collection and Use

Depending on the app, a Service may store a little non-personal data to do its job: preferences, game progress or settings. That data normally stays on your device. An app sends it to a server only if the app says so.

Some of my Services work fully offline and collect or send no data at all.

Data I May Collect

Where they apply, my Services may process these kinds of non-personal information:

  • App preferences and settings, stored on your device.
  • Game progress, scores and saved states, stored on your device.
  • Basic anonymized usage or crash data, which helps me improve the app.

I do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location, unless you give it to me yourself, for example when you email me for support.

Third-Party Services

Some Services use third-party tools such as app stores, hosting providers or basic analytics. Each of them follows its own privacy policy.

Advertising

Some of my apps may show ads from third-party ad networks. Those networks may use anonymized, non-personal information to pick relevant ads. I do not share personally identifiable information with advertisers.

Children's Privacy

My Services are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given me personal information, email me and I will take appropriate action.

Data Security

I take reasonable measures to protect the information my Services process. Data stored on your device is protected by your device and its operating system.

Your Choices

You can usually clear locally stored data by clearing your browser or app data in your device settings. Uninstalling an app removes the data it stored locally.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy. When I do, this page changes and the "Last updated" date changes with it.
